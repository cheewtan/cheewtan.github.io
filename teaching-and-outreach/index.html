Convex Optimization and its Applications in Computer Science

Theory, algorithms and software of convex optimization: cone programming (linear, quadratic and semidefinite programming) and geometric programming; decomposition, convex relaxation and proximal algorithms for large-scale and non-convex problems. Applications are drawn from graphs, Internet and wireless networks, social networks, machine learning and artificial intelligence.

Textbook: S. Boyd and L. Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, Cambridge University Press, 2004. Software: CVXPY (Stanford). Prerequisites: linear algebra, advanced calculus and some programming. I thank Professors Stephen Boyd (Stanford), Mung Chiang (Princeton) and Steven Low (Caltech) for many of the course materials.
